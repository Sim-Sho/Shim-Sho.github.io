Privacy Policy

App: Noise Vibe Spectrum  |  Developer: ShimSho  |  Last updated:

1. Overview

ShimSho ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Noise Vibe Spectrum iOS application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any personal data or information from our users. Specifically, the App does not collect or transmit:

  • Names, email addresses, or other identifying information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Microphone or accelerometer recordings

All sensor data (audio and vibration) processed by the App is handled entirely on your device and is never transmitted to any external server.

3. Third-Party Advertising

The App may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks (e.g., Google AdMob). These ad networks may collect certain non-personal, aggregated data as described in their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services integrated in the App.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. Changes are effective immediately upon posting.
